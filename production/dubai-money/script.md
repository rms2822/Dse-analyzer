# Dubai Money — narration script (90s YouTube Short, 9:16)

Record this straight through at a brisk, punchy short-form pace (~180-200s of
natural speech gets tightened in editing; aim to land close to 90s as spoken).

---

Dubai has almost no oil left — less than 1% of its economy runs on it. So how
does a desert city pull in 30 million tourists a year and host more
billionaires than most countries?

It starts with zero income tax, for individuals and, until recently, most
businesses too. That single policy turned Dubai into a magnet for global
wealth. Add over 30 free trade zones where foreign companies can own 100% of
their business, pay no corporate tax, and move profits freely — and you've
built the easiest place on Earth to park money.

Then there's the port. Jebel Ali is the busiest in the Middle East, moving
cargo between Asia, Europe, and Africa. Emirates and Dubai International
Airport turned the city into the world's connecting hub — 80% of the planet
is under eight hours away.

Real estate did the rest. The Burj Khalifa, Palm Jumeirah, entire artificial
islands — built to attract the ultra-rich, then sold to them, then taxed
through fees, not income.

Oil built the roads. Everything after that is what actually pays the bills.
