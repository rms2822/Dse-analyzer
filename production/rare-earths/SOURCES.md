# Sources for script.md's factual claims

Pulled via web search on 2026-07-05. Re-check anything time-sensitive before
publishing — export-control status in this space moves fast.

- China added 10 US companies (incl. MP Materials, USA Rare Earth) to its
  export control list, June 22, 2026:
  https://intellectia.ai/blog/china-rare-earth-export-controls-june-2026
- April 2025 controls (7 rare earths + magnets), October 2025 controls
  suspended for one year to Nov 10, 2026 (April controls + licensing
  infrastructure preserved), 5 more elements return to full control if the
  suspension lapses:
  https://discoveryalert.com.au/china-rare-earth-export-controls-processing-chokepoint-2026/
  https://informedclearly.com/en/geopolitics/54246/china-rare-earth-export-controls-2026
- China ~90-91% of global rare earth refining/processing, ~61-70% of mining,
  price spikes up to 6x, EU export license approval rates below 25%:
  https://www.iea.org/commentaries/with-new-export-controls-on-critical-minerals-supply-concentration-risks-become-reality
  https://rareearthexchanges.com/news/rare-earth-refining-bottleneck-why-china-leads-and-the-u-s-lags/
- China's share of sintered permanent magnet production ~94% today, up from
  ~50% two decades ago:
  https://rareearthexchanges.com/news/rare-earth-refining-bottleneck-why-china-leads-and-the-u-s-lags/
- Only a few industrial-scale rare earth refining facilities exist outside
  China: Malaysia (Lynas), a US facility, and one in Estonia:
  https://www.iea.org/commentaries/with-new-export-controls-on-critical-minerals-supply-concentration-risks-become-reality
- Deng Xiaoping's 1992 "the Middle East has oil, China has rare earths"
  quote, on his Southern Tour:
  https://dominotheory.com/how-a-deng-xiaoping-quote-became-china-watchers-favorite-anecdote/
- Mountain Pass mine (California): dominant global supplier 1950s-1980s,
  refining line shut down 1998, site closed after a toxic spill in 2002,
  reopened 2018, now ~47,000 metric tons/year (~10% of global supply):
  https://time.com/7294964/us-rare-earths-minerals-china-dominance-trump-tariffs/
  https://www.geopoliticalmonitor.com/a-brief-history-of-us-china-rare-earth-rivalry/
